About Adoni Kids Studio

Preserving the Delicate Emotion & Wonder of Childhood

Founded in Adoni, Andhra Pradesh, Adoni Kids Studio is a boutique fine-art atelier dedicated to capturing the fleeting, once-in-a-lifetime milestones of parenthood. From the quiet serenity of a 10-day-old newborn to the joyous laughter of first birthdays and timeless family portraits, we believe these delicate memories deserve to be preserved as generational heirlooms.

Unlike high-volume commercial studios, we deliberately host only one family at a time. Our private studio is purpose-built with temperature-controlled posing suites, medical-grade sanitized props, and peaceful nursing lounges—giving babies and parents an unhurried, gentle, and comfortable experience.

The Studio Difference

Why Families Trust Adoni Kids Studio

Every appointment is treated as an intimate celebration. We prioritize safety, comfort, and soothing patience above all else.

Hospital-Grade Hygiene

All wraps, blankets, and prop surfaces undergo medical-grade UV sterilization and non-chemical laundering before every baby's session.

Baby-Led Patience

We never rush a baby. Generous 2–3 hour session windows allow continuous pauses for soothing, nursing, diapering, and comforting cuddles.

Curated Wardrobe & Props

Complimentary access to designer maternity gowns, artisanal organic swaddles, handmade wooden bowls, and custom miniature themed sets.

Archival Keepsakes

Beyond digital files, we deliver museum-quality flush mount albums, bespoke framed wall art, and lifelike 3D metallic casts crafted to last generations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our unhurried, gentle, and bespoke photography sessions.

Newborn sessions take 2-3 hours, with breaks for feeding, soothing and diaper changes. Maternity, family and birthday sessions usually take 1-2 hours.

Ideally within the first 5-20 days after birth, when babies sleep most deeply. Book during pregnancy so your dates are held.

Just the baby's feeding essentials and a change of clothes. Wraps, props, and maternity gowns are provided. For family shoots, we share a colour guide before the session.

Retouched photos are delivered in 7-10 working days. Albums and frames take 2-3 weeks.
